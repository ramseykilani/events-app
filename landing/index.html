How it works

01

Person to person

Everything on it comes from someone you know — added by you, or shared straight to your calendar. Nothing is posted, and nothing is public.

02

The share is the ask

Sharing an event asks who's coming. A yes or a no finds its way back to the asker — from the app, or straight from the text.

03

Quiet by design

You hear from Events when a person does something, and never otherwise.
