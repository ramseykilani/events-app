Events

A calendar of events your people share with you.

You open the app. You see a calendar. On it are events your people have shared with you, on the days they're happening — each with a link to the public listing where you can buy a ticket or sign up. Want to pass one along to your own people? Tap share. Want to coordinate? You already have their number — text them.

There is no feed. There is nothing to scroll. No likes, no comments, no read receipts, no engagement metrics. The only notification the app ever sends is triggered by another person, never by the app itself.
